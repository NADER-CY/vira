# 14 — Independent Architecture Audit

**Status:** Phase 0 · 2026-10-28 · **Round 2 addendum in §6 · Round 3 (final consistency review) in §7** · **Advisory — approval is the product owner’s decision, not the architect’s or the auditor’s.**

## 1. Method

1. **Independent review.** A separate reviewer who had not taken part in writing docs 02–13 audited them cold against the brief, Master Requirements v0.1 and the UI manifest. The reviewer recomputed the inventory counts, contrast ratios, egress/storage arithmetic and the ledger worked example (all balance; closing trial balance ties out). It produced 30 findings: **2 CRITICAL, 9 HIGH, 14 MEDIUM, 5 LOW**.
2. **Remediation.** The architect corrected every finding that could be fixed on paper in this revision, and recorded where each fix lives. Findings that need the owner, counsel, a PSP contract or the original ZIP remain **OPEN**, each with a named gate.
3. **Verification.** After remediation, all 17 Mermaid diagrams were re-rendered with mermaid-cli, and cross-references (D-, ADR-, F-, phase numbers) were re-checked.

### What changed in the design because of the audit

* **Payment lifecycle:** the 15-minute checkout expiry no longer races the payment. Stock and orders are held until the intent is terminal or a PSP-derived deadline passes. All transitions are guarded under an intent row lock. Late captures have a defined path (T7).
* **PSP integration:** callbacks are treated as unsigned browser-mediated hints. Verify/settle is modelled per PSP. A verified payment is persisted before capture. “Already verified” codes are handled, units are explicit, and order numbers are numeric.
* **Ledger:** a complete posting-rule set (capture with shipping and both discount types, refunds before and after settlement and release, seller receivables, payout rejection and late bank returns) and a rounding rule. Platform aggregate accounts no longer have hot balance rows.
* **Database:** DB roles are per runtime (not per module), so the transaction scripts are possible. RLS is rewritten so buyers can browse and sellers are isolated without broad bypass. Partition keys are fixed. HA uses quorum sync and an off-provider copy.
* **Simplification:** JetStream is deferred. One outbox plus a PostgreSQL job queue serve Tier A; core NATS carries realtime only.
* **Security:** account recovery and recycled-number protection; Sheba-refund controls; KYC uploads through quarantine; admin API as a separate binary; CGNAT-aware rate limits; an external-dependency register.
