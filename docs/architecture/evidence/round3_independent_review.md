## 2. Verdict

**The package is close but not yet ready for final owner acceptance.**

The substance is sound:
* All 8 deliverables exist.
* The blocker list is precise, and future financial, crypto and advanced items are correctly kept out of it.
* Phase 1 is clearly NOT AUTHORISED.
* The USDT tracks, including two-way conversion and seller USDT withdrawals, are represented and gated.
* The seller dual-surface design is coherent.
* The cost arithmetic and unit prices check out.

Before acceptance:
* **Must fix (1 item):** F-01, the sequencing of the financial-model decision. It is the brief's central §3 requirement, and the package currently gives two different answers to when the model must be chosen.
* **Should fix (MEDIUM items):**
  * F-02, F-04 and F-05: these make the "superseded / not approved" status unambiguous.
  * F-03 and F-07: price and evidence provenance.
  * F-06: the seller permission boundary.
  * F-09 and F-10: the B-2 roadmap gap and the B-4 evaluation.
* After those edits, which are documentation-only and need about half a day, Phase 0 can be re-issued for final owner acceptance. F-11 should then be re-run as a quick consistency pass. The LOW items can be fixed in the same pass or carried as known errata.
