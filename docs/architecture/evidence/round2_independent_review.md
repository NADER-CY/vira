## 3. Overall assessment

1. The core factual claims hold. Integrity (389/389 hashes, identical nested ZIPs), theme counts, blank screenshots, Vazirmatn 162, 46 sidebars, zero cross-screen links, V-01 card capture, V-02 USDT default/recommended and V-03 credit are all reproduced, and 10/10 sampled §2 resolutions match the screenshots.
2. V-06 contains three refuted sub-claims (no second approver anywhere, no reason fields, 2FA only on RBAC) and a misplaced national-ID claim. V-04, V-05 and the sidebar crypto claim have attribution errors. These are wording and citation fixes; the dispositions stand.
3. Before sign-off, add M-01 (pre-ticked consents incl. save-card and biometric), M-02 (in-app fiat→USDT exchange) and M-03 (admin PAN search) to the findings. Correct V-06's wording.
